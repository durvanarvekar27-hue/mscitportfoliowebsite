Who We Are

Our Story

Kitty Shelter Home is a caring rescue and adoption center dedicated to helping homeless and injured cats find safe and loving homes.

Founded by passionate animal lovers, our shelter provides food, medical treatment, protection, and endless affection for rescued cats.

Every kitty deserves warmth, safety, and a forever family — and that’s exactly what we strive to give them every single day.

Our Mission 🐾

To rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome cats while creating awareness about animal welfare and responsible pet adoption. We believe every cat deserves love, safety, and a happy forever home.
